## Academic Explanation

### Preparing for Future Hackathons

A stronger preparation process can include:

1. Read eligibility rules, judging criteria, required deliverables, and deadlines early.
2. Translate each requirement into a checklist with an owner and due date.
3. Define the problem and value proposition before expanding the feature list.
4. Record ideas throughout development instead of waiting until the final stage.
5. Reserve time for testing, documentation, the demonstration, and submission review.

### Developing Serious AI Knowledge

Using AI tools is practical experience, but deeper capability also requires a structured foundation. A reasonable direction includes Python, mathematics and statistics, data preparation, machine learning, deep learning, model evaluation, deployment, and responsible AI. Huawei's public HCIA-AI materials describe the broader certification area as including programming, AI mathematics, machine-learning and deep-learning foundations, and practical AI application. This source context does not confirm the exact V4 syllabus studied on 20 July 2026.

The new data-quality topic is documented separately in [Data Quality in AI Learning](../learning/artificial-intelligence/data-quality-in-ai-learning.md).
